## Positions taken that a reviewer may want to overturn

Three properties are separated and reported separately: reconstructable, sound, correct. The component establishes the first, reports on the second, and is forbidden from assessing the third.

Thirteen citation roles, closed. The four that carry the weight and appear in no reviewed model are `CONTEXT`, `NEGATIVE_PREMISE`, `NON_RESULT_ACCEPTED` and `ALTERNATIVE_REJECTED`.

Reliance is a first class property of every citation admitting three values, distinguishing dependence from availability. This is the largest departure from W3C PROV, whose `used` relation is undifferentiated.

Frontiers: every chain terminus is a declared, kinded, justified stopping point, and `FRONTIER_UNDECLARED` is a defect. No reviewed standard provides this.

Five conditions of reconstructability, closed, assessed and reported separately from the single outcome member.

The completeness limit is stated as a requirement rather than left to inference: integrity mechanisms establish non alteration of what is present and do not establish that everything was recorded. Three mitigations are required and none is described as a solution.

Basis defect propagation enumerates every determination that relied on an artifact later found defective, and materiality is never computed. It defaults to `MATERIALITY_UNASSESSED` and requires a named person's act.

Nothing recorded ever changes state. Four state machines govern assertions about records, not the records themselves.

Accountability may not terminate in software. Third appearance of the position in the standard, and the counter argument is strongest here. Recorded open in 13.6.

## Research findings

**RFC 9943**, the SCITT architecture, published June 2026, after the author's general knowledge cutoff and established by search. Supplies issuer, signed statement, transparency service, append only ledger, receipt and auditor, and a threat model that does not assume a single trusted service. Sections 2.1, 3.17 and 10.3 depend on it. The full RFC text was not obtained; 13.1 flags it as the most load bearing source in the part.

**W3C PROV** family remains Recommendations of 30 April 2013 with no successor found. Three concepts borrowed deliberately: `actedOnBehalfOf` for delegation, the ternary Association with a plan for the method citation, and bundles for the recursion treatment at depth one.

**ISO/IEC 27037:2012** current, confirmed 2018, under review. Its four principles of auditability, repeatability, reproducibility and justifiability are the closest thing in any standard to a definition of reconstructability, and section 3.13 states the mapping. Its repeatability against reproducibility distinction is adopted as vocabulary and separates `Part 2`'s reproduction of a verdict from this part's condition five.

**NIST SP 800-92** is still the 2006 edition. Revision 1 has been an initial public draft since October 2023 and is narrower than what it replaces, excluding implementation and use of log data. Section 10.6 states the caution: log management and provenance share a vocabulary and are different problems, and the belief that the first satisfies the second is why a component like this is never built.

## Open, carried forward

Whether reliance can be honestly recorded, 13.2. Weakest point in the part. The mechanism is right and its population depends on something the specification cannot compel.

What counts as a determination, 13.3, delegated to the class registry and unconstrained.

The design against instance lineage boundary, 13.4, contestable in the same way.

Whether the completeness limit can be improved upon, 13.5. Mandatory receipts as a precondition of the determining act is worth serious examination and was not examined.

Whether the role set is right, 13.7. Closing it is the decision hardest to reverse, and a reviewer should test it against real determinations from at least three classes first.

Whether soundness assessment scales, 13.8. Class level assessment would cut the work by orders of magnitude and would permit exactly the blanket judgement the per determination requirement prevents.

The cost of the model, 13.9. Nothing was costed. One entry per citation including every context citation is the largest volume the standard requires anywhere.

## Gaps declared in 13.10

No mechanism compels a component to register. This part can refuse a defective registration and cannot compel a missing one, and the whole value depends on registration happening. Handed to `Part 0` and it is this part's largest dependency.

No privacy treatment. A basis routinely contains personal data, the reverse index reveals who determined what about whom, and the retention requirements are in tension with erasure. Substantive gap.

No cross organisational provenance, no treatment of very high determination rates.
