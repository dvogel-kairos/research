## Decisions taken that a reviewer may want to overturn

Lifecycle status and force state specified as two separate models rather than one status field.

Three clocks kept separate, with knowledge time always assigned by the component and occurrence time never assigned by it.

Fixity given five outcomes rather than two, discriminating mismatch from three indeterminate conditions.

A signer must be a natural person. An automated agent may author and may transition status, never sign. Recorded in 13.6 as a position, not a finding.

Effectivity scope requires an explicit assertion per scope, with no inheritance and no fallback to global.

Cryptographic erasure discouraged as a disposition method.

Retroactive effectivity permitted and flagged, distinguished from backdating an act, which is prohibited absolutely.
