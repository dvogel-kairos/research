## Positions taken that a reviewer may want to overturn

The rule is a proposition in the SBVR sense. The production sense of PRR and RIF-PRD is fenced into optional derivation; the decision table sense of DMN is allocated to Part 5.

Three valued logic with Kleene strong connectives, four normative truth tables, and Łukasiewicz rejected with the reason stated.

Seven verdict members in four classes, with satisfaction separated from inapplicability, violation separated from contradiction, and indeterminacy carrying one of five subclasses each with a named remedy owner.

Vacuity is a flag inside satisfaction rather than a verdict member. Recorded as open in 13.8, because a consumer that ignores the flag reproduces the failure the flag exists to prevent.

Enforcement level is declared on the rule version, never acted on here, and a change of level produces a new version. The last diverges from SBVR and is recorded in 13.5 as the most likely candidate for revision.

A model output may be a pinned input to a rule but a model may never be invoked during an evaluation.

An automated agent may author a rule and may not assert that the declaration expresses the statement. Recorded as a position in 13.7.

## Research findings

DMN's current formal version is 1.5, adopted August 2024, with betas 1.6 and 1.7 both dated September 2024 in process, so a citation to the latest DMN identifies a document the publisher marks informational.

ISO/IEC 19757-3:2020 Schematron is withdrawn. Edition 4, ISO/IEC 19757-3:2025, published September 2025, is current and already flagged for revision. It defines a validator as a function returning valid, invalid or error, which is the only normative anchor found anywhere for a three valued return type, and section 6.2 rests on it.

SHACL 1.0 of 20 July 2017 remains the Recommendation. The 1.2 family was in the technical report space through 2025 and 2026 with Core and SPARQL targeting Candidate Recommendation in January 2026; the maturity of each document could not be established.

SBVR 1.5 formal, December 2019. RIF-PRD Second Edition, W3C Recommendation, 5 February 2013, working group work complete. PRR 1.0 from 2009 with no evidence of the announced 1.1.

JSON Schema's current release is 2020-12 and its publisher still labels it a draft. The most widely deployed constraint language has no ratification, which is why section 9.3 requires an implementation to register its expression language semantics itself rather than by citation.

## Gaps declared in 13.13

No expression language is specified or registered. Deliberate, with the reason stated.

No rule over streams, aggregates across many subjects, or relationships between subjects. Every rule in the model is a proposition about one subject, so a rule bounding approvals per month is not expressible. This is the largest substantive gap.

No extraction method for rules embedded in an existing codebase, which is the first thing any adopter needs.
